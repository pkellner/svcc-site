The FREE and safely secured Ubuntu repository/app store has 30,000+ mostly FREE packages/apps.  &lt;b&gt;Would you like your app to be available or on sale for the 12 million Ubuntu users at the Ubuntu Software Center (ie. app store) also?&lt;/b&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;

.
