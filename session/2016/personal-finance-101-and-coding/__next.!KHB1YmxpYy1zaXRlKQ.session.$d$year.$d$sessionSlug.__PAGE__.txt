Financial Literacy at early age is a very important skill to have, since financial decisions that we take make great impact in shaping our lives.  The goal of this session is to contribute in building financially responsible and smart youth.

Finance part of the course will cover:
•	Basic money and finance related concepts
Programming part of the course will cover:
•	Procedural thinking

•	Programming fundamentals, such as control structures, variables
•	Testing and debugging programs to find issues
