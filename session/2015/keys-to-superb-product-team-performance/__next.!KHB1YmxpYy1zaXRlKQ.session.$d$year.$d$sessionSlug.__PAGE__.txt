2:T5ad,<p>Have you experienced the joy of being on a truly high-performing team? </p>

<p>It’s an experience we thrill to have been part of, an experience we only hope we can repeat in our careers. </p>

<p>But taking a team to that nirvana state is all too spotty and infrequent. </p>

<p>A group of us have been surveying teams about practices and approaches and ultimately about team performance for most of this decade. High performing teams are not only a joy to be part of, but also highly effective at delivering products that delight customers. </p>

<p>The 2012 Study of Product Team Performance revealed five practices that, if product teams are doing them, gives them a 67% likelihood of achieving high team performance. (Similarly, if they practice none of them, they'll have a miniscule 2% likelihood of achieving high team performance!). </p>

<p>The 2013 Study revealed rapid growth in the number of teams becoming agile - and fascinating insight into development methods and practices teams believe improve their products' profitability. </p>

<p>This year we discovered the correlation with standups. (Make them effective, hold them daily.) And with tightly integrating user experience. </p><p>Study co-author Ron Lichty will present the insights we gained from the 2015 Study of Product Team Performance - as well as key findings from earlier studies. </p>
