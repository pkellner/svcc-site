<p>Many of you have taken your kids to work and that's quite a proud and joyful feeling. Now bring your kids to a conference! <p>Shadaj Laddad will be sharing his experience of Scala programming and will build a game using HTML5 and Android. So sign up your kids for these exciting workshops and learn from some of the finest educators in the San Francisco Bay Area.</p>

Please check session abstracts for any software installation instructions prior to attending the workshop. Kids must be accompanied by a parent or an adult at all times.
