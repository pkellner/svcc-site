Code Camp Barely Two Months Away   

Mon Jul 25 2016
By Peter Kellner
Code Camp Coming Soon
Hi Past SV Code Camp Past Attendees,
 

In just two short months Silicon Valley Code Camp Version 11 will be happening again at Evergreen Valley College in San Jose on October 1-2 2016.  We have about half of our sessions already listed and we seem to be adding about 1 a day now.  Just in the last week or so we've added sessions include many repeat SVCC speakers including:

  • What is Python (Wesley Chun) 
  • Getting Started With Data Science (Dave Nielsen) 
  • Hacking Fitness with the Mean Stack (Daniel Egan) 
  • The Psychology of Developer Tools Usability (Sara Ford)
Not to mention sessions that have been up for a while including  Douglas Crockford (all previous 10 years!) talking about reinventing the internet, Gayle Laakmann McDowell has a new talk on how to be a better interviewer and Juval Lowy will be talking about being an awesome architect.
 
Register and pick your sessions of the ones we have up so far.
 
Silicon Valley Code Camp continues to be free because of our great sponsors and their financial support of us. Speaking of our sponsor line up, if you know of any sponsors that want to be involved in our event, please have them contact us (reply to this email or send to sponsors@siliconvalley-codecamp.com).  It's huge exposure for companies either looking to hire, promote their brands, get more exposure for the programming interfaces or just meet the 3000 or so of us.
