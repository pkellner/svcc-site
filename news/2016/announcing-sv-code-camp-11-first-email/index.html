Announcing SV Code Camp 11, First Email

By Peter Kellner

← back to all news

We sent our first big email announcement of SVCC 11 today. You can read the full content here including links to top speakers already with sessions posted.

Hi Past SV Code Camp Past Attendees,
 
Come to our 11th annual free* Silicon Valley Code Camp happening October 1-2, 2016.  Join our expected 2500+ attendees who are coming to learn what is the latest and greatest technologies from our awesome speakers and sponsors. Last year our 10th  Silicon Valley Code Camp brought multiple firsts.  The ones we are most proud of are:
  • First time we live streamed our main theatre all day Saturday (and published to YouTube)
  • First time we made the KTVU FOX 2 10PM Saturday News (45 second segment)
  • First time we made a big splash in the San Jose Mercury News
  • First time we brought in a professional photographer to memorialize our event
As always, we expect about 200 content filled sessions.  We already have four of our top speakers lined up with many more to come.
  • Douglas Crockford will be talking about the new web
  • Gayle Laakmann McDowell will be talking about careers
  • Ron Lichty and Greg Geracie will be talking about agile**
For this years Code Camp  (October 1-2, 2016) we plan on having at least one more first.  We are introducing a new Automotive Software track to recognize all the innovations that are coming to cars and how we as software and IoT people can get better involved. 
 
As always, space is limited so register now!  We are all looking forward to another great year.  Stay tuned.
 
Peter Kellner
https://peterkellner.net
Organizer
https://www.siliconvalley-codecamp.com (10/1-2/2016) and https://codestarssummit (9/29-30/2016)
 
*As always, we count on our awesome group of sponsors to foot the bill.  If you know of any company that can help us out, please send them to our sponsorship page or have them contact us at sponsorship@siliconvalley-codecamp.com and we will get back to them with how then can help.
 
 
**Silicon Valley Code Camp encourages you to participate in the fifth annual Study of Product Team Performance. The study authors Greg Geracie and Ron Lichty have presented their findings at Code Camp for each of the past four years.
