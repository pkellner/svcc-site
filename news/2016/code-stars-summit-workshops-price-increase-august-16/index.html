Code Stars Summit Workshops Price Increase August 16   

Mon Aug 15 2016
By Peter Kellner
<b>Workshops Workshops Workshops!</b>
 
 
Just a quick reminder that our earlybird pricing for Code Stars Summit Workshops ends tomorrow (workshops at Evergreen Valley College in San Jose). 
 
All our workshops are 1 and 2 days and are still available, so now is a great time to sign up.  Most classes are just 1 day (Friday 9/30) but a couple (SEO and Learning IOS and Swift) are 2 days (Thursday and Friday 9/29-30, 9AM to 4PM).
 
You can view more details on our pricing page in addtion to all the links below.
