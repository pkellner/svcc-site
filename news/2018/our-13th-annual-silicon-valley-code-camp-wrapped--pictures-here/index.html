Our 13th Annual Silicon Valley Code Camp Wrapped! Pictures Here   

Wed Oct 17 2018
By Peter Kellner
Having now wrapped 13 Silicon Valley Code Camps, words can't express my grattitude to our speakers, our volunteers, our sponsors, our host (PayPal) and of course all of our attendees for being part of all those events. This year in particular felt more like a family event, then a community conference. I kept hearing over and over how impressive we were as a team working together to deliver a truly memorable event. Thank you so much for being part of that.
