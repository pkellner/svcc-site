Registration Open! Oct 13-14 at PayPal Town Hall

By Peter Kellner

← back to all news

Registration is now open for Silicon Valley Code Camp happening Saturday and Sunday, October 13-14. Again, it will be hosted by PayPal at their Town Hall location in San Jose
 
Registration is now open for Silicon Valley Code Camp happening Saturday and Sunday, October 13-14.  Again, it will be hosted by PayPal at their Town Hall location in San Jose.
 
Returning speakers include Douglas Crockford (the inventor of JSON), Gayle Laakmann McDowell (talking about interviewing), Stephen Boesch (talking about statistics) and Nelson Petracek on Block Chain and IOT.  We currently have our first 22 sessions up.  Over the next 2 months, we will be adding 80 more so stay tuned.
 
Our venue is significantly smaller than our past community colleges where we had years with well over 3000 attending.  We expect to sell out as we did last year, so reserve your spot now to guarantee your admittance. Our early bird price is $25 and we'd just assume sell out at that price.
