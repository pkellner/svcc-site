Registration Opened Today! (July 9th 2019)   

Tue Jul 09 2019
By Peter Kellner
We opened registration today for our 14th annual Silicon Valley Code Camp. Early bird pricing is just $25 for the weekend!
Hi Code Campers,
 
Advanced ticket sales for Silicon Valley Code Camp 2019 start 9AM Tuesday July 9th  hosted again by PayPal.  Mark your calendars!
 
We’ve got an amazing event lineup with 50 sessions up so far. Log in to our new mobile site to pick the sessions you are interested in or do it through the tried and true sessions page we’ve had for years (I think you’ll like the new experience and it works great on mobile).  Our speakers love to hear that you are interested in their sessions.
 
Some highlights of what to expect:
 
  • Douglas Crockford, for year 14 of SVCC, will be talking this time about the History of JSON (which he invented)
  • IBM will be showing you how easy it is to program Block Chain among other tech
  • InterviewKickstart will be leading a panel of senior managers from top silicon valley companies on hiring
  • Agile and or Management Tracks Curated by Ron Lichty
  • React SPA Track curated by Benjamin Dunphy of Real World React
If you’ve not been to code camp in a while, check out our highlights reel from last year.  We are still limited to under 1000 people which means sign up early if you want to get a ticket.  Our early price is a nominal $25 for the full weekend.
 
See you October 19th!
