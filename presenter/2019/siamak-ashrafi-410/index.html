Speaking Sessions

  • iOS: The Best Parts

    3:45 PM Saturday   Room: Round Table
    We will cover all the new features of iOS development including Swift 5.1, ML Playgrounds, ARKit, and XCode10. This talk will include all the latest announcements from WWDC 2019. We finish the session by examining the similarities between Kotlin & Swift / Material Design & Apple HIG / Android Studio & XCode / Play Store & App Store. The similarities will shock you :-)

  • SciFi Comes Alive on Mobile

    9:15 AM Sunday   Room: Round Table

    Now that both Android and iOS have advanced sensors, Augmented Reality (AR) & Machine Learning (ML) frameworks shipping with their IDE we will see an explosion of life-saving medical apps, cool AR & useful ML technology.

    We start by showing some profound examples of the modern smartphone as a medical device. We explore how this is having an influence on the health of humanity. Digging in deeper we dissect how these apps are built and deployed.

    Then we learn what is AR and why it is considered the next big thing in tech by exploring the history and some mind-bending demos. After building a solid understanding of the what and why we start diving into the how. We will explore how to build an AR app on three different platforms: 1) iOS ARKit 2) Android ARCore 3) game engines Unity and Unreal. We will outline the advantages and disadvantages of each to help differentiate the different development processes.

    Next, we explain what is Machine Learning and how it is being used on mobile with TensorFlow Lite (Android) / Core ML (iOS). We will cover how ML works, how you build ML apps on mobile and what applications are being built to solve various problems.
