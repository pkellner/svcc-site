This session intends to provide a  overview of the interview process at these companies, and a guideline for anyone, especially self taught programmers, to have a fair chance at cracking coding interviews. 

Key sections covered in the session are overview of the interview process, strategies to get noticed by recruiters, topics to prepare for algorithms and data structures based coding interviews along with an overview of abstract topics like time complexity, recursion and dynamic programming, and strategies to answer behavioral questions.
