  • [Web 3.0] Smart Devices. Fun for Users &amp; Developers.

    5:00 PM Saturday   Room: 5001
    In this session we will explore the new world of ubiquitous, networked, mobile, smart devices and how it will shape our future. We will show where we were, where we are and where we might be going. We will cover Devices, Technologies, Ecosystem &amp; Applications / Development.
