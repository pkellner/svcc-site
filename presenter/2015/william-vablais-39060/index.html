Silicon Valley Code Camp : October 3rd and 4th 2015

William Vablais

Sr. strategy manager · Samsung Electronics

About William

With 25 years of experience in the computer industry as a hardware/software/systems engineer and migrating to business development, at McDonnell Douglas, Sun Microsystems and veteran of Microsoft, William’s passion and serial entrepreneurial drive have tuned his evangelist and business development abilities to start and manage new companies. He loves taking complex problems and concepts and distilling them into a simple understandable message without the drama. With several patents and industry technology standards that he developed in multimedia his interests have driven him to disruptive technology opportunities: mp3, p2p, mobile etc… Within the last decade his efforts brought him closer to the telecoms industry, building out businesses in virtual pbx systems, voice command and recommendation engines. He is a passionate inventor with a focus on the realities of building and scaling businesses around new technologies and markets. Before moving to Samsung, he managed BlackBerry’s EMEA Developer Relations Group. Now at Samsung Research America, based out of Mt. View, he is focused on business development and building relationships with B2B developers worldwide.
