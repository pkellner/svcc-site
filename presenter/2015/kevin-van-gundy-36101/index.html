Speaking Sessions

  • When Graphs Can & Tables Can't: an Introduction to Neo4j & Graph Databases

    3:30 PM Saturday   Room: AD-211
    This is a brief introductory session on what graph databases are, how they can be used, and what purposes that they serve. During this session, Kevin will present, explain and discuss introductory topics that require no prior knowledge on graph databases at all. There will be a quick discussion around what graph databases are and why they matter. From there, Kevin will dive into key data modeling techniques and Neo4j's Cypher Query Language.
