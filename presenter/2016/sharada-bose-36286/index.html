About Sharada
Sharada Bose is the COO at Way.com, an online marketplace for services. After a successful career at HP as a technology leader, she has embraced the fast paced, versatile world of start-ups. She is now building a company that is connecting the Connected Driver to simple services with IoT technology .

  • The Service Industry enters the Connected Car

    Analysts are predicting that the Connected Car is going to revolutionize the world like the mobile phone did 10 years ago. By 2020 it is predicted that 20% of the car industry's revenue is going to come from sales of products and services via the With the rapid adoption of Internet of Things (IoT), bringing services to the Connected Car is the next logical step.Many service focused companies are involved in making location-based services search-able, order-able and pay-able, all with a single touch on the Connected Car dashboard. This session will review the state of the services industry and discuss how this is going to disrupt the way we have thought about the automobile dashboard.
