What is Bebo?<br /><br />

Bebo is a platform for people to carve out private spaces for the various groups in their life. <br /><br />

How it works is simple: <br /><br />

1. You push a button, to create a private server.<br />
2. You choose who has access and what rooms are on the server<br />
3. You can install a chat room, a photo room, a game room. Any type of room, <br />
